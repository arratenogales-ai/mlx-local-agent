# Skill: academic summary

Summarize a long text into a faithful, concise **academic summary**. This is a MODEL task: there is
no mechanical part. The text to summarize is the one the user provides (in their message).

## Rules

1. **Fidelity first.** Do not invent data, figures or conclusions that are not in the text. If the
   text does not say something, do not add it. Do not give opinions: summarize what is there.
2. **Academic structure.** Start with the **thesis/objective**, continue with the **key points or
   findings** and close with the **conclusion**. Formal, impersonal prose (avoid "I think", "in my
   opinion").
3. **Concise.** By default, 4 to 6 sentences or about 120 to 200 words (fewer if the text is short).
   If the user asks for a specific length, respect it.
4. **No copy and paste.** Rephrase in your own words; do not lift long sentences from the original.
   Keep **technical terms** exact.
5. **Light context.** Work only with the given text; do not request or invent external sources.
6. **No meta-commentary.** Return **only the summary**, with no "Here is the summary:" and no
   closing remarks.

## Example

**Text (excerpt):** "The study analyzed 240 samples over 18 months. Treatment A was found to reduce
symptoms by 32% compared to placebo, although with mild side effects in 12% of cases. The authors
conclude that A is promising but requires larger trials."

**Summary:** "The study evaluated treatment A on 240 samples over 18 months. A reduced symptoms by
32% relative to placebo, with mild side effects in 12% of cases. The authors consider it promising,
though they note the need for larger trials."
